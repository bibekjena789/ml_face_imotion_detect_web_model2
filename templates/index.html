Terms & Conditions (version {{ terms_version }})

  1. This site uses your camera to detect faces and predict facial emotions in real time.
  2. While a face is in view, a picture of your face is saved about once per second, together with your name, the time and the predicted emotions.
  3. These pictures and predictions are stored on this server and may be used to review and improve the emotion models.
  4. Predictions come from machine-learning models and can be wrong. A facial expression doesn't always show what someone actually feels, so don't use the results to make decisions about anyone.
  5. Only use your own face, or the face of someone who has also agreed to these terms.
  6. You can stop at any time with the Exit button. To have your saved pictures deleted, contact the site owner with the name you entered.
